# Style Calibration for Mathematical Prose

Use this reference when preserving a user's style, matching a venue, or improving prose without flattening the author's voice.

Style calibration is not anti-AI laundering. It must not hide uncertainty, remove proof signposts, weaken assumptions, or make claims broader than evidence supports.

## Priority order

1. Mathematical truth and source accuracy.
2. Checkable proof structure.
3. Field and venue conventions.
4. User or author voice.
5. Local elegance.

## Calibration corpus

Use only text that is appropriate to imitate:

- accepted papers by the same author;
- polished sections the user identifies as representative;
- target venue papers when author style is unknown;
- local project prose that has already passed mathematical review.

Do not imitate drafts that contain known mathematical, citation, or reproducibility errors.

## Dimensions to profile

- sentence length and rhythm;
- paragraph length and transition density;
- theorem/proof roadmap style;
- how definitions and assumptions are introduced;
- citation integration habits;
- hedging and limitation language;
- notation exposition;
- abstract/introduction contribution phrasing;
- experiment-description density;
- preferred verbs for mathematical action.

## Safe style features

Safe to imitate:

- cadence of short vs long sentences;
- placement of motivation before formalism;
- degree of signposting;
- citation grammar;
- compactness of theorem commentary;
- preference for examples before or after definitions.

Unsafe to imitate without correction:

- vague novelty language;
- missing assumptions;
- citation dumping;
- excessive compression of proof logic;
- unsupported superiority claims;
- hidden experiment conditions;
- overuse of rhetorical intensifiers.

## Calibration output

Produce a style profile with:

- corpus and scope;
- stable features;
- forbidden imitations;
- revision rules;
- a small before/after sample when useful.

During revision, report any place where preserving style conflicts with accuracy.
