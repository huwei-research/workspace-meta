# Playbook: Numerical Experiment Audit

1. Identify the experimental question.
2. Identify compared methods and baselines.
3. Identify metrics and success criteria.
4. Check reproducibility details.
5. Check randomization, repetitions, and aggregation.
6. Check table/figure suitability.
7. Check whether the text separates objective statements from interpretation.
8. Check extrapolation beyond tested instances.
9. Check whether the abstract/introduction overclaim experimental evidence.
10. Suggest safe wording.

Output uses `assets/experiment_reproducibility_template.md`.
