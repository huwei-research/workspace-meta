# Playbook: Draft a New Mathematical Paper

1. Extract the reader contract.
2. Build a contribution ledger.
3. Build result cards for main theorems, algorithms, examples, and experiments.
4. Draft a section outline whose order minimizes reader resistance.
5. Draft the introduction only after the result cards are clear.
6. Draft theorem statements before proof prose.
7. Draft proof roadmaps before technical details.
8. Mark unsupported citations and claims.
9. Produce a revision plan rather than pretending the first draft is final.

Output:

1. Assumptions
2. Reader contract
3. Contribution ledger
4. Proposed outline
5. Draft text
6. Required sources or proof details
7. Revision plan
