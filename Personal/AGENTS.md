# Personal Category Instructions

## Scope and Inheritance

Read `../AGENTS.md`, relevant `../CONVENTIONS.md` sections, and project-local
instructions where present. This category file belongs to `workspace-meta`;
personal contents remain excluded from that repository.

## Structure and Versions

- Distinguish independent personal repositories from local documents, context,
  transfer inventories, workspace-management reports, and skill backups.
- Use the relevant personal project's existing source, asset, and export layout.
  Do not impose manuscript, solver, or formal-experiment folders on personal work.
- Keep one editable authority per language or purpose; identify exported copies
  and dated backups. Use Git history in repositories and dated provenance in
  manual-transfer folders. Do not rewrite backup instructions as current policy.

## Privacy

- Preserve factual accuracy in CVs, identity materials, and personal records.
- Keep sensitive documents, personalized skill briefs, local inventories, and
  unpublished project-status notes private. Do not publish them in workspace-meta.
- Follow the project's sync policy and `../TRANSFER_GUIDE.md` for trusted
  manual transfers. Directory cleanup does not authorize publication or deletion.
- Do not inspect unrelated personal document contents during workspace maintenance.
